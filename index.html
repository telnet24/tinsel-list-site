Tinsel List

Christmas planner and gift list for iPhone. Coming to the App Store.

A Christmas planner a family keeps installed all year. Save present ideas the moment they come up - a photo in a shop in July, a typed "wants a new bike", a screenshot shared in from any app - filed under the person who wants them. When you are ready, one tap moves an idea into the buying plan, tracked through ordered, arrived and wrapped with a budget for every person.

  • A Christmas card list that renews itself: addresses stay, ticks reset.
  • Face ID lock, so presents stay secret on a borrowed phone.
  • Received presents and thank-you notes, ticked off as they go out.
  • A flip-clock countdown that switches to presents still to buy as the day nears.
  • Everything on your device. No accounts, no ads, no tracking.
