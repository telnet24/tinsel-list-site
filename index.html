Your Christmas list, from idea to wrapped.

Remember who you’ve bought for, what’s still on the way, and how much you’ve spent. All together on your iPhone.

One-time purchase. No subscription.
For iPhone with iOS 17 or later.

For everyone you’re buying for.

Save a gift idea with a photo or note and keep it with the right person. When you’re ready to buy, move it into your Christmas plan.

Keep the little details close at hand, from a present you’ve hidden away to a thank-you you still need to send.

Know what still needs wrapping.

Mark gifts purchased, ordered, arrived, wrapped and given as you go. See what’s left to do for each person without searching through notes.

See what you’ve spent.

Set a budget for each person. Add estimated prices while you plan, then record what you actually paid. See your spending and planned purchases together.

Example plan shown in British pounds.

Keep next year’s card list.

Make a card list for the people you write to, store their addresses, and mark cards sent. Keep track of received gifts and thank-yous too.

Keep the presents a surprise.

Tinsel List works offline and needs no account. Add an optional Face ID or passcode lock to keep presents private. Encrypted backup lets you take your plans to a new phone.
